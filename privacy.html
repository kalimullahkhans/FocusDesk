Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains exactly what information FocusDesk collects, how it is used, and what third parties (including Amazon) may receive when you use this site.

Information we collect

FocusDesk does not require registration and does not collect names, email addresses, or payment details directly. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send and use it only to reply.

Cookies and affiliate tracking

When you click a product link on this site, you are sent to Amazon.com. Amazon may place a tracking cookie (typically lasting up to 24 hours, and longer for items added to your cart) that tells Amazon the visit came from this site. This is how the Amazon Associates program attributes qualifying purchases and pays commissions. As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases. We do not control these cookies — they are governed by Amazon's Privacy Notice.

Third parties

How we use information

We use any information we receive solely to operate and improve the site — for example, understanding which guides are helpful. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; affiliate attribution may stop working, but the site remains fully usable. You may also contact us to ask what limited information we hold about you (for example, an email you sent).

Children's privacy

This site is directed at adults and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy
