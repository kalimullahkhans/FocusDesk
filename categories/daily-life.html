When to actually buy

Timing beats loyalty in this category — the same product can move 30% across a year.

Best months to buy

January and late August — back-to-routine periods when accessory brands clear stock. Discounting is steady the rest of the year, so do not wait long for a few percent.

What to skip

Anything sold in a 12-pack. Bulk accessory bundles rarely beat buying the one item you need, and the quality per unit drops.

How to spot a fake discount

If a listing shows a permanent 50%-off badge all year, that is the real price. Judge the price against the two or three comparable listings, not against the crossed-out number.

Before you order

  • Read the one-star reviews first — they describe the failure mode, which is more useful than praise.
  • Check whether the warranty is handled by the seller or the brand; marketplace sellers disappear.
  • For anything under $30, returns matter more than durability — confirm free returns before ordering.
