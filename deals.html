The discount calendar

When each category actually gets cheap. Timing beats loyalty — the same product can move 30% across a year.

January

Post-holiday clearance. Beauty gift sets, daily essentials and home organisation drop hardest — retailers clear rather than store. Best month for small items.

April–June

Summer stock lands before demand spikes. Fans, curtains and cooling buy cheapest here, not in July when everyone is searching.

August–September

Summer clearance plus back-to-routine. Desks, chairs and accessories discount as offices restock — the second-best window of the year.

November

The deepest month overall. Furniture and electronics cut hardest, but gifting sets are already discounted — buy them now, not in December.

Late January–March

Winter clearance. Heaters, throws and humidifiers are cheapest exactly when you still need them, and stock lasts longer than December.

Never

Do not buy anything with a permanent "50% off" badge. When a discount never ends, the crossed-out price is fiction.

Black Friday timing

Desk and chair discounts move in a predictable staircase every November — and the pre-sale window usually beats Cyber Monday on stock.
