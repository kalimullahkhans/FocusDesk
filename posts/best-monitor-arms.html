How we evaluated

We compared build materials, motion quality (gas-spring vs static), weight capacity, VESA compatibility, clamp/grommet options, warranty terms, and verified owner feedback — with particular attention to sag-and-drift complaints, the classic failure mode of budget arms.

Two mistakes that ruin a monitor-arm buy

Arms without gas-spring tensioning (fixed-height poles) if you ever change positions, and any arm whose weight rating is close to your monitor's actual weight — leave headroom or it will slowly sink.

Frequently asked

Will an arm fit my monitor and desk?

Check three things: your monitor's VESA pattern (almost all are 75×75 or 100×100), its weight against the arm's rating, and your desk's edge thickness for the clamp. All three are listed on the product page.

Do monitor arms damage desks?

A properly torqued clamp with its pad won't. Grommet mounting (through an existing hole) is even cleaner if your desk has one.
