How we evaluated

We compared sensor size and type, low-light behavior, sharpness at real call resolutions (most apps cap at 1080p), microphone quality, mounting flexibility, and verified owner feedback on long-term reliability. Note: none of these replaces good lighting — a cheap lamp beats an expensive sensor in a dark room.

Marketing traps to skip

No-name 4K webcams with interpolated (fake) resolution, and any webcam without a proven brand behind its driver support — discontinued drivers are how webcams die. Also skip "4K" claims if your call app caps at 1080p; sensor quality matters more than the number.

Frequently asked

Do I need 4K for video calls?

No — almost every call platform compresses to 1080p or less. You're paying for the better sensor that comes with 4K cameras, not the 4K itself.

Webcam or phone camera?

A phone with a good camera mounted properly can beat budget webcams — but it's clumsy for daily work. The C920x exists precisely to make this question disappear.
