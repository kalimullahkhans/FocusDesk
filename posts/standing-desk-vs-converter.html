The short verdict: if your current desk is fine and you only want to occasionally stand, buy a converter — it's the cheaper, zero-waste option. If you'll stand daily and want a clean, stable, spacious setup, a full standing desk is worth it. Don't buy a converter expecting a full desk experience; don't buy a full desk if you already love your desktop surface.

The 30-second decision rule

Buy a converter if: you stand less than ~30% of the day, you rent or move often, or your current desk is something you love.

Buy a full desk if: you stand most of the day, you run monitor arms or multiple screens, or your current desk is aging anyway — then a converter is a half-step that costs you twice.

Frequently asked

Can a converter hold dual monitors?

Most can hold two monitors on the top deck, but weight limits are lower than a full desk and stability suffers. If dual monitors are permanent, a full desk is the better home for them.

Do converters damage the desk underneath?

Quality models have padded feet and won't scratch. The bigger risk is overloading a lightweight desk — check both the converter's and your desk's weight ratings.
