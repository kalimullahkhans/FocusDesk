How we evaluated

We compared adjustability range (lumbar, armrests, recline), build materials, warranty terms, and verified owner feedback — especially complaints filed after 6+ months of use, where cheap chairs reveal themselves. Where we haven't personally long-term tested a chair, we base the ranking on documented specs and owner experiences, never invented claims.

The "ergonomic" trap: what to avoid

Chairs that advertise "ergonomic" but offer only a thin lumbar cushion bolted to a static backrest. If the lumbar support doesn't adjust, it isn't ergonomic — it's decoration.
