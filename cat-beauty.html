When to actually buy

Timing beats loyalty in this category — the same product can move 30% across a year.

Best months to buy

November, then January. Beauty discounts hardest in Black Friday week and again in post-holiday clearance, when gift sets are broken up and clearanced.

What to skip

Anything with an unstated active ingredient, and 'value' bundles of sample sizes. Also skip devices with no replaceable parts and no stated wavelength or wattage.

How to spot a fake discount

Beauty has the highest rate of invented list prices. Check the per-ml price against a comparable product — that number cannot be faked by a badge.

Before you order

  • Check the expiry or PAO symbol — discounted stock is sometimes short-dated.
  • For devices, confirm the plug type and voltage for your country before ordering.
  • Patch-test anything with an active ingredient, especially retinol and acids.

Questions people ask

Is discounted beauty stock old stock?

Sometimes. Check the period-after-opening symbol and the batch date in reviews. Short-dated stock is the usual reason a set is 50% off.

Do LED masks actually do anything?

The technology has clinical backing at specific wavelengths and doses. Cheap masks often under-deliver both, so a stated wavelength and safety certification are the minimum.

Why is beauty discounted so deeply?

Shelf life and seasonality. Brands would rather clear stock than carry it, which is why 30–50% appears far more often here than in furniture.
