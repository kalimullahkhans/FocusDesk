When to actually buy

Timing beats loyalty in this category — the same product can move 30% across a year.

Best months to buy

Electronics discount on release cycles, not holidays: the deepest cuts hit when a successor launches. For tool and leather gifts, November and Father's Day periods are strongest.

What to skip

Novelty gadgets, cheap drone-style toys and anything that needs an app nobody maintains. Also skip tool sets where the case costs more than the tools.

How to spot a fake discount

Last-generation electronics often carry a permanent 'was' price. Check the model number: if it launched three years ago, the discount is the normal price.

Before you order

  • Confirm the model number and generation — listings blend old and new versions under one page.
  • For electronics, check the warranty region; grey imports are not covered locally.
  • Check whether accessories (case, cable) are included; many 'deals' strip them out.

Questions people ask

Headphones or earbuds?

Over-ear for desk and travel comfort, earbuds for commuting. If he already owns one, upgrade the other rather than duplicating.

Is a multi-tool a safe gift?

Yes, if it is legal to carry where he lives — locking blades are restricted in several countries, including parts of the UK and EU.

What about personalised gifts?

Engraved leather or a monogrammed wallet adds perceived value for very little cost, and it discounts less because it is made to order.
